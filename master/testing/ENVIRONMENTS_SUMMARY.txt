COMMON ISSUES & FIXES
=====================

❌ ISSUE: Low completion rate overall
   → Increase rerouting aggressiveness
   → Lower REROUTE_HEALTH_THRESHOLD (40 → 35)
   → Increase latency penalty (-35 → -45)

❌ ISSUE: Good on SANDBOX, bad on HARD/EXTREME
   → Your thresholds are hardcoded to sandbox numbers
   → Use relative signals, not absolute
   → E.g.: "if error_rate > avg_error_rate * 2" not "if error_rate > 0.5"

❌ ISSUE: High churn (too many reroutes)
   → Raise REROUTE_HEALTH_THRESHOLD (40 → 45)
   → Increase reroute cooldown (5 → 7)
   → Require higher evidence before rerouting

❌ ISSUE: Times out on EXTREME
   → Your decision logic is too slow (O(n²) or worse)
   → Reduce history window (5 → 3)
   → Precompute once per step, not per task

KEY METRICS TO TRACK
====================

1. COMPLETION RATE (Most Important)
   = completed_count / (completed_count + failed_count)
   Target: 75%+ on SANDBOX, 70%+ on MEDIUM, 60%+ on HARD

2. DETECTION LATENCY (Medium Importance)
   = Steps until no new assignments to a failed node
   Target: < 5 steps

3. CHURN RATE (Least Important)
   = # of unnecessary reroutes (where node was actually healthy)
   Target: Minimize (only reroute with evidence)

================================================================================

SCORING FORMULA (Reference)
============================

Score = completed * 1 + failed * (-1)

Example 1: 80 completed, 20 failed
  Score = 80 - 20 = 60  |  Completion rate = 80/100 = 80%

Example 2: 60 completed, 40 failed
  Score = 60 - 40 = 20  |  Completion rate = 60/100 = 60%

Example 3: 75 completed, 25 failed
  Score = 75 - 25 = 50  |  Completion rate = 75/100 = 75%

Baseline (no health awareness): ~50 score, ~55% completion

================================================================================

WHEN TO USE debug=True vs debug=False
======================================

debug=True:
  ✓ Shows info["node_true_states"] (ground truth health)
  ✓ Shows info["failed_this_step"] (exactly when failures occur)
  ✓ Good for learning and understanding failures
  ✗ NOT available during evaluation (don't rely on it)

debug=False:
  ✓ Simulates real evaluation (no ground truth)
  ✓ Only obs["nodes"] telemetry available
  ✓ This is what your agent must handle
  ✗ Harder to debug, but necessary for validation

RECOMMENDATION:
  • Development: debug=True (watch ground truth)
  • Validation: debug=False (simulate real conditions)
